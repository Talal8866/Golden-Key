Who We Are

Dubai's Premier Locksmith Company

Golden Key Lock Repairing EST is a professional locksmith company established since 1989 in Karama, Dubai. We provide exceptional, complete locksmith services to clients in dire need across the UAE.

We have been serving our teaming clients in Dubai and its environs as their top-tier, go-to locksmith company for about three decades. Our unparalleled experience cannot be matched — Golden Key and our technicians are insured and fully licensed, so our clients could have absolute trust in us.

As a company, we are dedicated to providing efficient and quality service at an affordable price. We are known for our swift response and efficient service delivery. Our commitment to quality sets us apart from other locksmith businesses.
